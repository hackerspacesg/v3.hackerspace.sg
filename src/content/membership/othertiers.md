### Concessionary Tier

We have a Concessionary tier for individuals earning under $2,500/month, such as full-time students, NSFs, entrepreneurs just starting out, underemployed, and unemployed. The value of this tier is daytime access — great if you need a workspace during the day but can't afford a full membership.

The Concessionary tier is priced at $64/month, and includes 9 hours/week access (including daytime) and 22L of storage. We may ask for proof of eligibility.

If you prefer to trade labour for membership, we have a [Barter Membership](/barter) programme. Barter Members represent HSG to visitors, so we're selective — there's an in-person chat and a trial period before onboarding.

### Supporter Tier

If you'd just like to hang out in the evenings, check out our [Supporter tier](/support) at $365/year ($1/day!). Supporters get access to the space from 7pm to 11pm daily — the social hours when the kampong comes alive.
